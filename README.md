# credit-risk-analysis-sql.-

## Questions answered

**1. Portfolio overview**
- How big is the loan portfolio (total exposure, average, smallest and largest loan, average duration)?
- How much account information is missing?

**2. Customer segments**
- How do loan count, exposure and average loan differ across age bands?
- What share of loans do women and men hold, and how do their average loans compare?
- Does the gap between women and men change across age bands?
- Which housing groups hold the most exposure?
- How do loan size and duration change with job skill level?

**3. Exposure by purpose**
- Which loan purposes make up the biggest share of total exposure?
- Which purposes have a larger average loan than the portfolio average?

**4. Financial buffer**
- How do loan size and duration vary with savings level, including unknown savings?
- How many large, long loans went to customers with little or no savings and checking balances?

**5. Risk score**
- Which loans have the highest risk scores and should be reviewed first?
- How are loans and exposure split across Low, Medium and High risk tiers?
- Which purposes have the highest share of High-risk loans?
- Do women and men differ in average risk score?

**6. Ranking and window functions**
- What are the 3 largest loans within each purpose?
- Which loans are furthest above their purpose average?
- Which loans carry the heaviest estimated monthly repayment?
- How concentrated is the exposure in the largest 10% of loans?

**7. Summary**
- Which combinations of age band and housing status carry the highest average risk?
